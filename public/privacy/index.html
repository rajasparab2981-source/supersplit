SplitPro

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026 · Applies to the SplitPro web app at supersplit.netlify.app

In short: SplitPro stores the groups, members and expenses you add so you and your friends can split costs. We use your Google or email sign-in only to identify you. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use tracking or analytics tools. You can ask us to delete your account at any time.

1. Who we are

SplitPro (“SplitPro”, “we”, “us”) is an expense-splitting app operated from India. For any privacy question or request, email splitpro.app@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

When you sign in

  • With Google: your name, email address and profile picture URL, as shared by Google. We do not get your Google password and we do not access your Gmail, contacts, Drive, calendar or any other Google data.
  • With email: your email address, used to send you a sign-in link.

What you add to the app

  • Profile: your display name and, if you choose to add it, your UPI ID.
  • Groups and trips: names, emoji and colours you choose.
  • Members: the names of people in your groups. You can add a friend by name before they join; they can later claim that name with their own account.
  • Expenses and payments: description, amount, date, category, who paid, how it is split, reactions, and payments you mark as settled.
  • Photos: receipt or other photos you choose to attach to an expense.
  • Activity history: a record of changes made in a group (for example, who added or edited an expense) so members can see what changed.

Technical information

  • On your device: the app stores your sign-in session and a few preferences in your browser's local storage, and caches the app's files so it opens quickly. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.
  • Service logs: our hosting and database providers keep standard technical logs (such as IP address, browser type and request times) to run and secure the service.

3. How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To show your groups, calculate balances and suggest who should pay whom.
  • To keep the same data in sync across your devices and with the members of your groups.
  • To fix problems, prevent abuse, and respond to your requests.

We do not sell or rent your information, use it for advertising, or use it to build profiles about you.

4. Who can see your information

  • Members of your groups can see your display name, the group's expenses, payments and photos, the activity history, and your UPI ID if you added one (so they can pay you).
  • People outside your groups cannot see your groups or expenses. Someone with a valid invite link can see only the group's name and the names they could claim before joining.

6. Google user data

SplitPro uses Google sign-in only to identify you. It requests your basic profile (name and profile picture) and email address, and uses them only to create and display your SplitPro account. We do not transfer this information to others except as needed to provide the service (our providers above), do not use it for advertising, and do not allow humans to read it except with your consent, for security, or as required by law.

SplitPro's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. How long we keep it

  • Your account and profile are kept while you use SplitPro.
  • Group data is kept while the group exists, because other members rely on it to know who owes whom. Deleted expenses and payments are hidden from the app; they may remain in the group's history.
  • When you ask us to delete your account, we delete your account, profile and sign-in details within 30 days. Expenses you took part in stay in the group's records so other members' balances remain correct, but they are no longer linked to your account.
  • Photos are deleted when their group is permanently deleted, or on request.

8. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and similar laws where you live, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and how it is used;
  • correct or update it (you can also change your name and UPI ID in Settings);
  • delete your account and personal data;
  • nominate someone to exercise these rights for you if you are unable to;
  • withdraw your consent, by deleting your account.

Email splitpro.app@gmail.com from the address linked to your account. We reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

9. Security

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Access rules in our database ensure that only members of a group can read its data, every change is checked on our servers, and photos are kept in private storage that is shown only through short-lived links. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify you and the authorities as required by law if a breach affects you.

10. Children

SplitPro is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.

12. Contact

Questions, requests or complaints about privacy: splitpro.app@gmail.com.
